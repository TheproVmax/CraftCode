About

What is CraftCode?

CraftCode is a coding tutoring program built around learning through practice. Classes focus on understanding concepts, solving problems and creating projects instead of simply memorizing code.

From Scratch to AI coding, the goal is to make programming understandable, useful and creative for students at different skill levels.

Questions

Do I need experience?

No. Courses can be approached at different learning levels. We can discuss your experience when you enquire.

Who are the classes for?

Students with different ages and skill levels. Contact us to discuss whether a course is a good fit.

What happens in a class?

Classes include explanations, demonstrations, practical exercises and coding challenges.
